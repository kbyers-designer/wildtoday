Biodiversity

# Biodiversity ## Overview This includes diversity within species, between species and across ecosystems, representing the genetic makeup of plants, animals, microorganisms and the complexity of ecosystems. Healthy communities are sustained by well-functioning ecosystems, which provide critical services such as clean air, fresh water, natural medicines and food security. These ecosystems also regulate diseases and help stabilize the climate

What Is Biodiversity?

## Key takeaways - Biodiversity’s role: It keeps ecosystems stable and resilient, supporting food, water, clean air, medicine, and protection from natural disasters. ## Biodiversity protects our future All species and organisms in an ecosystem work together, like an intricate web, to maintain balance and support life. Everything in nature is connected and plays a role in keeping our ecosystems healthy and functioning
